注音故事 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 20 日

以下說明適用於「注音故事」iOS 1.2 與 visionOS 1.2，涵蓋故事生成、注音閱讀、朗讀、離線辭典與插圖功能。

我們處理哪些資料

App 主要處理你在裝置上輸入或選擇的故事條件，例如主題、風格、議題方向、閱讀偏好，以及你所產生或儲存在 App 內的內容。

故事、插圖與系統服務

1.2 的故事文字生成使用 Apple Foundation Models 的裝置端系統模型，需支援的裝置、系統設定與已下載模型。App 已移除外部文字模型與 API 金鑰設定，不會將新的故事生成請求轉送至原有的外部文字供應商。

建立插圖時，App 將故事相關描述交給 Apple Image Playground 系統介面處理。iPhone／iPad 的選用語音輸入會在使用時要求麥克風與語音辨識權限。系統生圖及語音服務的資料處理與網路需求依 Apple 的系統功能、設定與隱私說明而定；本頁不表示所有系統功能均完全離線。

儲存與離線使用

故事、插圖與閱讀資料保存在裝置上的 App 儲存空間。已儲存故事的閱讀與內建教育部辭典查詢可離線使用。開發者不蒐集你的故事內容；裝置備份則依你的系統設定處理。

從舊版更新

1.2 保留既有故事與插圖，並清除 App 原有外部模型功能的設定及其持有的 API 金鑰。這項更新不會刪除過去已傳送給外部服務的資料；若你曾在舊版使用外部模型，該次處理仍適用當時服務提供者的條款與隱私政策。

聯絡方式

隱私相關問題請來信：mibsteven.chang@gmail.com

注音故事 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This information applies to Zhuyin Story iOS 1.2 and visionOS 1.2, covering story generation, Zhuyin reading, narration, offline dictionaries, and illustrations.

Information We Process

The app mainly processes story options you enter or select on your device, such as topic, style, curriculum theme, reading preferences, and content you generate or store inside the app.

Stories, Illustrations, and System Services

Version 1.2 uses Apple Foundation Models for on-device story text generation, requiring a supported device, system configuration, and downloaded model. External text providers and API key settings have been removed. New story requests are not forwarded to the former external text providers.

When creating an illustration, the app passes story-related descriptions to Apple's Image Playground interface. Optional speech input on iPhone/iPad requests microphone and speech-recognition permissions when used. Processing and network requirements for system image and speech services depend on Apple's features, settings, and privacy information. This page does not claim that every system feature works entirely offline.

Storage and Offline Use

Stories, illustrations, and reading data stay in the app's storage on your device. Saved stories and the built-in Ministry of Education dictionary can be read and searched offline. The developer does not collect your story content. Device backups depend on your system settings.

Updating from Earlier Versions

Version 1.2 preserves existing stories and illustrations while removing the retired external-model settings and API keys held by the app. Updating does not erase information previously sent to an external service. If you used an external model in an older version, that earlier processing remains subject to the provider's applicable terms and privacy policy.

For privacy-related questions, contact: mibsteven.chang@gmail.com
