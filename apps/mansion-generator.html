從一組條件，繼續發展你的空間概念。

設定基地尺寸、面積、樓層與廳房衛需求，搭配風格和 seed 生成可重現的設計。生成在裝置本機完成，不需雲端 AI 生成或 AI token。你可以保存方案、比較差異，再局部套用先前的設定。

把模型帶進下一個創作工具。

依用途輸出完整住宅、僅外觀或可編輯分件，包含 OpenUSD、USDZ、SVG 平面圖與診斷資料。這支影片的外觀運鏡、客廳與藝品照片，就是使用 App 的匯出模型，在 Blender 加上燈光與攝影機製作。

適用於概念設計與空間原型。生成成果不是建築、結構、消防、法規或施工圖說；專業用途需由合格專業人員審查。

A set of conditions becomes a spatial concept.

Set the site dimensions, area, floors, and room needs, then choose a style and seed to generate a repeatable design. Geometry is generated locally on your device, without cloud AI generation or AI tokens. Save designs, compare changes, and reapply selected settings from earlier versions.

Take the model into your next creative tool.

Export complete homes, exteriors, or editable parts as OpenUSD and USDZ, with SVG plans and diagnostics. The exterior animation and interior photographs above use actual app exports, with lighting and cameras set in Blender.

For concept design and spatial prototyping. Generated results are not architectural, structural, fire-safety, code-compliance, or construction documents. Professional use requires qualified review.
