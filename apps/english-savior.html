學習流程

1. 嘗試表達

輸入中文想法和自己的英文版本，保留真實練習痕跡。

2. 拆解修正

查看提示、答案解析與朗讀內容，知道哪裡可以更自然。

3. 延伸閱讀

用累積單字生成故事、插圖與理解題，讓練習可以回顧。

主要功能

語音輸入與朗讀

支援語音輸入，並可朗讀提示、答案、口說文本與故事。

互動字典

閱讀故事時可點選英文單字查詢解釋與例句。

跨平台學習

iOS 適合行動練習，visionOS 則整理成更沉浸式的學習空間。

Learning Flow

1. Try expressing it

Enter a Chinese idea and your own English version so practice starts from your real attempt.

2. Break it down

Review hints, answer explanations, and read-aloud text to understand what sounds natural.

3. Extend into reading

Use saved vocabulary to generate stories, illustrations, and comprehension practice.

Key Features

Voice and read-aloud

Use voice input and listen to hints, answers, speaking text, and stories.

Interactive dictionary

Tap English words in stories to view explanations and example sentences.

Cross-platform learning

Use iOS for mobile practice and visionOS for a more immersive study space.

AI and Privacy

English Saver uses On-Device AI by default and supports BYOK options for Gemini and Groq external text models. The app does not collect personal data through developer-operated servers. If you configure an external model, data handling follows that provider's policy.

When you know what you want to say but the English does not feel natural yet, English Saver gives you a repeatable practice flow.
