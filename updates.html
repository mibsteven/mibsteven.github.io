Spatial Electronics Lab 建立完整的空間電子學習路徑

這款開發中的 Apple Vision Pro 教材把虛擬電腦、相容開發板、麵包板與電子元件放到混合實境工作台。學習者可以在不同個人檔案中，沿用前一堂完成的電路，依序練習安全點亮 LED、程式閃爍、按鈕輸入、光敏電阻量測、自動夜燈與 PWM 平滑調光。每個主題都包含繁中／英文語音示範、逐步練習、即時電路檢查與獨立測驗；Code Studio 會分開保存草稿與已上傳程式，並顯示診斷、序列、類比與 PWM 訊號。短路、極性反接、浮接輸入、錯誤腳位與 LED 過電流會依實際拓撲產生可觀察、可復原的結果，而不是只跳出答案。核心課程與進度都留在裝置上，App 不會自行連接外部 AI 服務；下一步是 Apple Vision Pro 實機的手勢、音效、可讀性、放大操作與多導線效能驗收。

08.29Development

Spatial Electronics Lab forms a complete spatial learning path

This Apple Vision Pro project places a virtual computer, compatible development board, breadboard, and components on a mixed-reality workbench. Separate learner profiles can carry completed circuits forward through a safe LED, programmed blinking, push-button input, photoresistor measurement, an automatic night light, and smooth PWM dimming. Each topic combines Traditional Chinese and English narration, guided practice, live circuit checks, and an independent assessment. Code Studio keeps drafts distinct from uploaded firmware while exposing diagnostics, serial output, analog readings, and PWM telemetry. Shorts, reversed polarity, floating inputs, incorrect pins, and LED overcurrent create observable and recoverable consequences from the real circuit topology instead of only displaying an answer. Core lessons and progress stay on device, and the app does not contact an external AI service by itself. The next milestone is Apple Vision Pro hardware validation for gestures, audio, readability, magnification, and multi-wire performance.
